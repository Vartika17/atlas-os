# Anti-patterns

Always loaded in full at critique (it stays short — an anti-pattern list
longer than ~20 items means principles are failing upstream). Each entry:
what, why, and the tell.

## AI-flavored prose

Filler cadence ("in today's fast-paced world"), triads everywhere, hedged
claims, "delve/leverage/unlock". **Why:** reads as mass-produced; erodes the
honesty principle. **Tell:** sentences that could appear in any business's
content unchanged.

## Borrowed authority

Design or copy that imitates a famous brand's surface (Apple-style minimalism,
luxury-brand tropes) without the substance behind it. **Why:** invites a
comparison we lose. **Tell:** "make it look like X" appearing anywhere in the
brief.

## Feature-first selling

Leading with what the product is instead of what the customer's day looks
like after. **Why:** violates specificity + audience-first; personas don't buy
features. **Tell:** first paragraph contains a spec list.

## Fake urgency

Countdown timers, "only 2 left", manufactured deadlines not grounded in
reality. **Why:** short-term lift, long-term trust erosion — the wrong trade
on a 20-year horizon. **Tell:** urgency claim with no operational cause.

## Unearned "we"

Case studies, testimonials, or claims implying scale/history the business
doesn't have. **Why:** honesty principle; also legal exposure. **Tell:**
plural achievements in a solo business's copy that knowledge/ can't back.
