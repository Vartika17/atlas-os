# Personas — Nazaria

<!-- Validation-stage personas: hypotheses to be corrected by the first ten
sales conversations, not demographic fiction. -->

## Farida — the outgrown founder

- **Situation:** 2-5 year old business (services or products), revenue real,
  brand still the DIY logo from launch week.
- **Pain:** looks smaller than she is; loses deals to worse competitors who
  look bigger; embarrassed sending the deck.
- **Buying trigger:** a lost deal, a launch, or an investor/partner meeting
  on the calendar. [VERIFY against real conversations]
- **Objections:** "designers burned me before"; "how is this different from
  a cheap logo gig"; "I can't articulate my brand, isn't that your job?"
- **Language:** "look professional", "premium but not fake", "something
  that lasts".

## Imran — the second-generation upgrader

- **Situation:** family business passing to the next generation; product is
  proven, presentation is decades stale.
- **Pain:** the brand repels the customers the business now needs; internal
  resistance to change ("father's logo").
- **Buying trigger:** generational handover, new location, or entering a
  modern channel (e-commerce, exports). [VERIFY]
- **Objections:** "our customers know us as we are"; cost; fear of losing
  existing recognition.
- **Language:** "modernize without losing ourselves".
